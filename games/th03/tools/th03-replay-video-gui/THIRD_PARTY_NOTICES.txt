TH03 Replay Renderer embeds the libth03 replay renderer, which incorporates
98fmplayer, ymfm, and TinySoundFont under their respective licenses. Package
builds include those license texts.

The GUI uses Python, Tcl/Tk, PyInstaller, tkinterdnd2, and TkDND. The package
builder copies their applicable license texts into the licenses directory.

FFmpeg is not included by default. If a distributor supplies an FFmpeg binary
to build.ps1, they must also supply the matching license text; both become
part of that package and the distributor remains responsible for compliance.
